# Movadorichee Business Model

## Revenue Streams

### 1. B2C (Business-to-Consumer) Delivery Services
- **Standard Delivery**: $5-15 per package
- **Express Delivery**: $15-30 per package
- **Same-Day Delivery**: $25-50 per package
- **Subscription Plans**: $9.99-29.99/month

### 2. B2B (Business-to-Business) Solutions
- **Enterprise Contracts**: Custom pricing based on volume
- **API Integration**: $500-5,000/month
- **White-Label Solutions**: Custom packages
- **Bulk Shipping Discounts**: 20-40% off standard rates

### 3. Premium Services
- **Scheduled Delivery**: +$3-5 per delivery
- **Fragile Goods Handling**: +$5-10 per package
- **Temperature-Controlled**: +$10-20 per package
- **Insurance Options**: +1-5% of package value

### 4. Data & Analytics
- **Delivery Analytics Dashboard**: $99-999/month
- **Business Intelligence Reports**: Custom pricing
- **Market Insights Subscription**: $199/month

## Cost Structure

### Fixed Costs (Monthly)
- **Facilities & Infrastructure**: $200,000
- **Technology & Systems**: $100,000
- **Administrative Staff**: $150,000
- **Marketing & Sales**: $80,000
- **Rent & Utilities**: $50,000
- **Total Fixed**: ~$580,000/month

### Variable Costs (Per Delivery)
- **Driver Compensation**: $2-5 per delivery
- **Vehicle Fuel/Electric**: $0.50-1.50 per delivery
- **Vehicle Maintenance**: $0.25-0.75 per delivery
- **Packaging Materials**: $0.50-2.00 per delivery
- **Customer Support**: $0.25-1.00 per delivery
- **Payment Processing**: 2.5-3.5% of delivery value
- **Total Variable**: ~$4-12 per delivery

## Risk Management

### Operational Risks
- Driver shortages: Partner with gig platforms
- Vehicle breakdowns: Preventive maintenance program
- Regulatory changes: Compliance team monitoring

### Market Risks
- Competition: Differentiate through sustainability and tech
- Customer acquisition cost: Focus on retention
- Pricing pressure: Diversify revenue streams

### Financial Risks
- Cash flow: Secure line of credit
- Debt service: Conservative growth
- Market downturn: Flexible cost structure
